Details
Plays at 48 kHz instead of 24 kHz. An upsampler adds the high frequencies, so the voice sounds brighter and clearer. It takes extra computing time, so on first use it is switched on only if this device is fast enough. You can change it at any time.
A small detector listens while the speech is made. Where the pitch suddenly jumps to another note, that moment is made again before it plays. On slow devices there is not always time for it.
